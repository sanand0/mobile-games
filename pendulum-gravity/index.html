03 / Run the experiment

Time the swings

Safety first. Only swing a phone with adult supervision, inside a fully closed, securely fastened pouch attached to a strong string and fixed support. Test it gently over a soft surface, away from faces, people and breakables. Never use a charging cable or loose phone strap. If unsure, choose Stopwatch instead.
  1. While holding your phone, tap Check sensor and gently tilt it. Allow Motion access if asked.
  2. Put the phone in a snug pouch, attach it securely, and measure pivot-to-phone centre distance.
  3. Tap Start countdown. You have 8 seconds to get into position. At the long beep, release from a small angle (under 15°) without pushing.
  4. Let it swing in one plane. Keep the screen awake. At the ending beeps, gently catch it.

Works with motion sensors on supported phones. No data is sent anywhere.

Safer choice: leave your phone on a table. Hang an eraser or small weight from a string on a fixed support. Ask a friend to operate the stopwatch.

  1. Measure the length from the pivot to the centre of your hanging object.
  2. Pull it less than 15° to one side; release without a push.
  3. Tap Start as the bob passes an extreme position. Count 10 complete back-and-forth swings. Stop when it returns to that same side for the 10th time.

One full oscillation: left → right → left. Not just left → right!

Where does the number come from?

A longer pendulum takes more time. The relationship is T = 2π √(L / g). Rearrange it to calculate g = 4π² L / T². Here L is in metres and T in seconds.

This assumes a short swing, a nearly point-like bob and a fixed pivot. Air resistance, sideways movement, loose attachments, length errors and stopwatch reaction time affect the result.
